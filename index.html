FMC Archive: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Friends Meeting of Cambridge · Last updated October 2026

What this is

The FMC Archive is a tool for members and attenders of Friends Meeting of Cambridge (FMC). It helps you open the Meeting's historical minutes and reports from your own computer.

What the tool asks Google for

During setup, the tool asks Google for permission to work with only the Google Drive files and folders you choose with it. It uses that permission once, to add a shortcut named "FMC Archive" to your own Google Drive that points to the Meeting's archive folder. It does not ask to see, change or delete any of your other files.

What we collect and keep

Nothing. The tool does not send your information to FMC or to anyone else. It does not store your Google sign-in, your email address, or anything from your Drive. The Google permission is used during setup and is not saved by the tool. You can withdraw it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Reading and adding records

The archive page runs on your own computer. It reads the archive folder you point it to. If you use Add a record, it saves the text you provide as a file in that folder, and Google Drive for desktop then uploads it to the Meeting's shared folder, the same as any other file you put there. The page does not send your searches or anything you read to any server.

The archive itself

The archive folder is shared with members by FMC. Whether you can open it is controlled by Google Drive sharing, which FMC manages. Some historical records contain sensitive personal information about named people. Please treat what you read as confidential to the Meeting and do not copy or share it outside the Meeting without checking with the Meeting.

Terms

The tool is provided as a service to the Meeting, as is, with no warranty. FMC may change or stop it at any time. By using it you agree to use the archive only for the Meeting's purposes.
